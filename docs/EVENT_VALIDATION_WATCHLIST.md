## 2027 F1 dates to monitor

The [official 2027 F1 calendar](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2027), published 16 Sep 2026, lists these **EU** rounds: Portugal 18–20 Jun; Austria 9–11 Jul; Belgium 23–25 Jul; Hungary 30 Jul–1 Aug; Italy 3–5 Sep; Spain 10–12 Sep. It separately lists **Türkiye / İstanbul Park 1–3 Oct 2027**. Monaco and Great Britain are European races but are not EU member-state cases. Dates are race-weekend dates in each venue's local timezone; closure times, transport plans and measured effects must be verified separately.

F1 is a candidate only where the roads and independent observations match the model's question. Dedicated circuits such as İstanbul Park, Portimão, Spielberg, Spa, Hungaroring and Monza may mainly disturb access corridors; do not assume a city-center road closure. Madrid's 2027 race may offer a different urban-access setting, but no road restriction or outcome is pre-validated by the calendar alone. Run a coverage/rights feasibility audit before building a CityPack or scoring a prediction.

## Reminder and freeze protocol

- At first notice, preserve the dated primary page and source hash; label scheduled versus actually operated restrictions separately. For Berlin, the 21 Sep official notice has been preserved locally on 24 Sep with SHA-256 `a83157701a57001f1d93e24fe0869fc7936a13264bef51357f715b8d9ee4abd9`. The first announced closures were already active when this copy was saved. The 26/27 Sep incremental forecast still needs a recorded freeze before its relevant restrictions begin.
- About seven days before each event, check the route, directed closure segments, time windows, vehicle classes, event-date OSM/GTFS coverage, detector/route locations, access rights and a same-weekday unaffected control set. Report missing coverage rather than substituting a city-wide average.
- By the day before the first closure, freeze the city snapshot, source-to-edge review, scenario/action hash, graph policy, SUMO demand/seed, predicted physical outputs, candidate universe and **held-out** observation plan. Predictions frozen after seeing the outcome are exploratory replay, not prospective validation.
- One day after the event, collect actual operations and timestamped directional measurements from an independent source. Within seven days, score V1/V2/V3 separately with denominators, units, missing observations and negative controls. Do not reinterpret PPR as a travel-time or safety prediction.
- Watch official pages for date/route changes. A changed date or geometry requires a newly versioned manifest and reminder; do not silently mutate a frozen run.

No Google Maps traffic, route or imagery content is imported into this MapLibre/OSM pipeline; see the [license/data note](HISTORICAL_BACKTEST_PROTOCOL.md). Do not start paid API calls, book tickets, or contact authorities as part of this watchlist.
